Privacy Policy

Social Manager — Social Media Management App

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

SocialFlow ("the App", "we", "our") is a social media management tool that helps users schedule, generate, and publish content — including AI-generated captions and images — to Facebook Pages they personally administer. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, and how it is protected.

2. Information We Access

When you connect your Facebook account to SocialFlow, we may access:

  • Basic profile information (name, profile picture) needed to identify your account
  • A list of Facebook Pages you administer
  • Page access tokens, used solely to publish posts on your behalf to Pages you manage
  • Content you create within the App (post text, images, captions), including content generated via AI (Google Gemini) at your request

3. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate you and connect your Facebook Pages to the App
  • To publish posts, images, and captions to Pages at your explicit direction
  • To generate AI-assisted captions and images based on your prompts, using the Google Gemini API
  • To display your connected Pages and post history within your own SocialFlow dashboard

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage & Security

Access tokens and content are stored securely and used only to perform actions you initiate (such as publishing a scheduled post). We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Third-Party Services

SocialFlow integrates with the Meta Graph API (for Facebook Page publishing) and the Google Gemini API (for AI caption/image generation). Your use of these integrations is also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy and Google's applicable API terms.

6. Data Deletion

You may disconnect your Facebook account from SocialFlow at any time via your Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, which revokes the App's access to your Pages. To request deletion of any data SocialFlow has stored about you, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

SocialFlow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
